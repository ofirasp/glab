Find your way around the guitar

The thin high E string is at the top, and the thick low E is at the bottom. Open means fret 0. Each fret raises the pitch by one semitone; fret 12 repeats the open note one octave higher.

Explore: tap a note to hear it. Highlight a note to find its positions. Include ♯ / ♭ to show all twelve pitches. A scale filter automatically includes its sharps or flats; dim dots are outside that scale.

Intervals: R means root, 3 means major third, ♭3 means minor third, and 5 means fifth, all relative to Root / key. These labels describe the sound of the note in the key.

Find the Note: tap any visible position with the requested note name, in any octave. Names are hidden until you answer. Hear target gives an audio hint. Correct answers move to the next question. Changing the fret range or filter chooses a new question. This is a touchscreen quiz, not microphone recognition.
