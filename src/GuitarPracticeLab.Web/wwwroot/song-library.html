Song tools

A — · B —

Use −5s / +5s or the song position slider to find a passage. Set A at its start, B at its end, then turn Loop on.

Chord match

Use headphones so the mic hears your guitar. Match the chord on screen in the recording’s key. Each chord section counts once. Play from start to finish without seeking to save a personal best.

Analyze chords

Compare the original MP3 with chord names found in the main PDF. The detected chord appears above the PDF as the track plays and stays visible until the next chord is detected.

If a sheet says G but the recording sounds A, select +2. A capo at fret 2 commonly needs this shift.

Record & Review

Use headphones so the mic hears your guitar clearly. Takes are up to 60 seconds. Download the WAV to keep it.

Song rhythm

Analyze the original MP3 for a tempo and a possible time signature. Listen and adjust before saving.

Replace PDF

The song title, original MP3, backing track and notes stay. A different PDF clears Follow cues because its pages may have changed. Selecting the exact same PDF keeps the cues.

Delete this song?

This removes this entry and its attached PDF, audio, notes and cues from the library. Other entries and files in recordings stay. A recovery copy is kept on the Pi.

Song library

Import a song

On the Pi, a matching -backing.mp3 in the recordings folder is attached automatically when you choose -original.mp3. From a Mac, select both MP3 files together.

For Simple version, put the matching -simple.pdf in the Pi recordings folder and import the standard PDF.

You can import several PDFs together and attach each recording later. PDFs: up to 20 MB / 100 pages. MP3: up to 100 MB / two hours.

Song cues

A cue links the current recording time to a position on the PDF. Tap a cue to jump there; your reading zoom is preserved. Record Follow starts from the current playback position and captures where you stop scrolling or turn a page. Tap or hold ↓, then pause for one second to record a mark.

Review Follow recording

Remove any unwanted marks. Saving adds the remaining marks to your existing cues. Marks at the same time as an existing cue are skipped.

Mark this section

Pause the song and scroll the PDF to the start of the section before marking it. Follow will keep that page position at your chosen zoom.
